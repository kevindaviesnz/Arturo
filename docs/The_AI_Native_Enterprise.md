# The AI-Native Enterprise

## A New Operating Model for Businesses Built Around Artificial Intelligence

### Working Draft v0.2

## Summary

This paper asks:

> What would a company look like if Humans and AI were designed to work
> together from the first day?

It organises the company around lasting abilities called Business Functions.
People, AI models, and tools can change while purpose and responsibility stay
visible.

This is a design paper, not a list of current Arturo features. New readers
should begin with [Arturo in plain English](./START_HERE.md).

# Foreword

Every generation of technology changes the way organisations operate.

The Industrial Revolution mechanised physical labour.

The Information Age digitised information and communication.

Artificial intelligence introduces a different kind of change. It does not merely automate individual tasks; it enables work itself to be delegated to software that can reason, adapt, and collaborate.

Many organisations are attempting to incorporate AI into operating models that were designed decades ago. They add AI assistants to existing departments, introduce isolated automation projects, or embed language models into established workflows. These efforts can deliver meaningful productivity gains, but they leave the underlying organisation largely unchanged.

This paper argues that the greatest opportunity lies elsewhere.

Rather than retrofitting artificial intelligence into legacy organisations, we believe the next generation of companies will be designed around AI from their first day of existence. These organisations will not think of AI as a tool used by employees. Instead, AI will become an integral part of how the organisation itself operates.

We refer to these organisations as **AI-native enterprises**.

This paper presents a conceptual operating model for such enterprises. It introduces Business Functions as the primary organisational building block, distinguishes enduring organisational capabilities from replaceable workers and technologies, and proposes an architecture for enterprises that continuously operate, remember, measure, and improve themselves while remaining under meaningful human governance.

The goal of this document is not to describe a software product.

Its goal is to describe a new organisational architecture.

One possible implementation of that architecture is Arturo.

The ideas presented here, however, are intended to stand independently of any particular implementation.

If successful, they should remain relevant even as today's AI models, tools, and programming frameworks are replaced by tomorrow's.
