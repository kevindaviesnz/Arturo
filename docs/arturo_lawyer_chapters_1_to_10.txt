ARTURO THROUGH THE EYES OF A SOLE-PRACTICE LAWYER
Chapters 1–10 companion walkthrough

STATUS
------

This is a synthetic teaching example. It is not legal advice.

Alex Morgan and Harbour Legal are fictional. Never use real client information
in an Arturo evaluation.

The maintained chapter guide is docs/README.md. Exact command help is available
inside Arturo with /help <command>.


THE SCENARIO
------------

Alex is starting a small legal practice called Harbour Legal.

Alex remains responsible for:

* client confidentiality;
* conflicts;
* legal competence;
* Human review; and
* professional judgement.

Arturo does not become a lawyer. An AI Worker has no legal qualification or
authority.


CHAPTER 3 — CREATE THE ENTERPRISE
---------------------------------

/enterprise create "Harbour Legal" --mission "Provide careful and accessible legal services to individuals and small businesses."

/enterprise update "Harbour Legal" --description "A fictional sole-practice example used with synthetic data."

/enterprise inspect "Harbour Legal"

Alex may ask Arturo for suggestions:

/next "Harbour Legal"

The suggestions are not changes until Alex accepts them. Alex should review
each suggestion and may answer no.


CHAPTER 4 — CREATE ONE BUSINESS FUNCTION
-----------------------------------------

/function create "Matter Intake and Acceptance" --enterprise "Harbour Legal" --objective "Decide whether a synthetic prospective matter is suitable for review."

/function update "Matter Intake and Acceptance" --description "Uses synthetic intake details to demonstrate suitability and conflict questions."

/function inspect "Matter Intake and Acceptance"

This Function describes an enduring ability. It is not one client matter.


CHAPTER 5 — START THE RUNTIME
-----------------------------

/function start "Matter Intake and Acceptance"

/function inspect "Matter Intake and Acceptance"

Starting a Runtime does not give it access to email, files, or real client
systems.


CHAPTER 6 — ADD WORKERS
------------------------

/add-ai-worker "Legal Intake Analyst" --archetype analyst

/add-human-worker "Alex Morgan" --archetype guardian --availability available

/workers

/workers inspect "Legal Intake Analyst"

These records describe roles in the fictional exercise. Registration does not
prove identity, employment, or professional qualifications.


CHAPTER 7 — CREATE AND INSPECT WORK
------------------------------------

/task-cap --capability analysis --bounded-task "Using only the supplied fictional intake note, list missing information and assumptions. Do not give legal advice or claim a conflict check was completed."

/jobs

Copy the Job ID:

/job <job-id>

A completed Job means the Worker returned output. It does not mean the output is
correct, complete, or safe to use.


CHAPTER 8 — USE MEMORY CAREFULLY
---------------------------------

/memory add "Synthetic intake exercises must request all relevant party names before demonstrating a conflict-review step." --scope "Matter Intake and Acceptance"

/memory

Do not store client names, case facts, privileged material, or personal data.
A Human-entered Memory entry is still an assertion, not independent proof.


CHAPTER 9 — INSPECT EVENTS
---------------------------

/events

/timeline

Events and Timeline records help Alex understand what Arturo did. They are not
a complete legal file or professional audit.


CHAPTER 10 — READ HEALTH HONESTLY
----------------------------------

/health "Matter Intake and Acceptance"

/function inspect "Matter Intake and Acceptance"

Unknown Health may mean there is not enough measured evidence. Completed Jobs
alone do not prove good legal outcomes.


FINAL SAFETY CHECK
------------------

Before any real legal use, Alex would need legal, privacy, security, identity,
data-separation, supervision, and professional-rule review. This private
evaluation example does not provide those controls.
