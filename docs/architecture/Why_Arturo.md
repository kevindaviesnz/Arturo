# Why Arturo?

> **Summary:** Arturo exists to give AI work clear company
> ownership, limits, Human authority, and an inspectable history.

Arturo is named after conductor Arturo Toscanini. The name captures the product's central idea: Arturo coordinates specialised performers toward an organisational outcome without pretending to perform every role itself.

## The conductor analogy

An orchestra contains performers with different skills. The conductor does not play every instrument. The conductor establishes timing, coordinates dependencies, resolves the shape of the performance, and remains aware of the whole.

Arturo similarly coordinates:

- enduring Business Functions that own organisational outcomes;
- Runtimes that evaluate signals and create warranted work;
- replaceable AI and Human Workers;
- bounded Jobs and Delegations;
- deterministic policies and approvals;
- Events, Operational Memory, Health, and audit history.

The analogy has limits. Arturo is software, not an accountable executive. It does not possess legal authority, professional qualifications, organisational intent, or independent access to evidence merely because it can coordinate work.

## Why not call it an agent framework?

An agent framework usually begins with agents and asks how they collaborate. Arturo begins with the organisation:

```text
Mission → Enterprise → Business Functions → Runtimes → Jobs → Workers
```

Workers are execution resources. They can be replaced as models, people, tools, and suppliers change. Business Functions persist because the organisation still needs their outcomes.

This separation prevents four common mistakes:

1. naming a model as if it owns a business outcome;
2. embedding responsibility in a temporary workflow;
3. confusing generated prose with verified evidence;
4. allowing action without an explicit policy and authority boundary.

## What Arturo conducts today

The current reference implementation can define Enterprises and Functions, start Runtimes, route Jobs by capability, publish Events, retain provenance-labelled Memory, compute operational Health, enforce Governance and approvals, create Human Tasks, delegate within an Enterprise, and provide read-only Worker chat.

It does not thereby gain access to external systems. Connectors, credentials, policies, review, and explicit authority are still required. Cross-Enterprise Delegation, autonomous external communication through chat, and native Artefact management remain outside the current primary terminal surface.

## The design principle

Arturo should make the organisation more understandable as automation increases. A user should be able to determine:

- which Function owned the work;
- why a Job existed;
- which Worker performed it;
- what evidence supported it;
- which policy applied;
- who approved or rejected it;
- what changed afterward.

Arturo is not the orchestra and it is not every performer. Arturo is the governed coordination layer that helps the Enterprise act as a coherent system.
