Airport Closure due to Fog

Scenario Overview
A regional airport is expected to close for the morning due to dense fog, affecting approximately 200 scheduled flights.

Options for Rebooking and Accommodation Considerations

1. Rebook Passengers on Next Available Flight: Reschedule passengers onto the next available flight, potentially leading to longer travel times and increased costs.
2. Offer Alternative Airports or Routes: Provide passengers with alternative airport options or routes that may be affected by fog but are less congested.
3. Compensate Passengers for Travel Disruptions: Offer compensation to passengers for inconvenience caused by the closure, such as vouchers or meal allowances.

Vulnerable-Passenger Duty of Care

1. Notify Vulnerable Passengers in Advance: Inform vulnerable passengers (e.g., young children, seniors) about the airport closure and arrange for alternative arrangements, if possible.
2. Provide Support for Special Needs: Offer support for passengers with special needs, such as accessible transportation or accommodations.
3. Maintain Communication Channels: Ensure that all passengers are informed about the situation and any updates through designated channels.

Cost and Reputation Trade-Offs

1. Minimize Cost Increases: Balance the need to accommodate affected passengers with the need to minimize cost increases for rebooking services.
2. Prioritize Passenger Experience: Invest in reputation management efforts to mitigate potential negative impacts on Aotearoa Air Ops' brand.

Decisions for Duty Manager

1. Assess Severity of Disruption: Evaluate the impact of the closure on passengers and operations, determining the level of support required.
2. Coordinate with Airport Authorities: Collaborate with airport authorities to assess the cause of the closure and potential solutions.
3. Communicate with Stakeholders: Keep stakeholders informed about the situation and any updates through regular communication channels.

Illustrative Example
In a similar scenario, Aotearoa Air Ops offered vouchers for meal allowances to passengers affected by the closure, while also providing support for vulnerable passengers. The airline invested in reputation management efforts to minimize potential negative impacts on its brand.

Next Steps
To further develop this analysis, additional data would be needed, such as:

–  Passenger demographics and special needs requirements
–  Airport infrastructure and capacity limitations
–  Operational costs and revenue implications
–  Industry standards for rebooking and compensation practices
