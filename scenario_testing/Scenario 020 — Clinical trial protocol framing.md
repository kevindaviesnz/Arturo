Phase-1 Protocol Hypothetical Framework

In framing the ethics and safety questions for this hypothetical Phase-1 protocol, we must prioritize the well-being and autonomy of participants while ensuring the integrity of the research.

Inclusion/Exclusion Uncertainty

1. How will we identify and recruit participants who may be at higher risk of adverse events or may be more susceptible to inclusion/exclusion criteria?
2. What measures will we take to ensure that our sampling strategy is fair, equitable, and representative of the target population?
3. How will we account for potential biases in the selection process and address them proactively?

Consent Risks

1. What are the potential risks associated with obtaining informed consent from participants, particularly those who may be vulnerable or have limited understanding of the research?
2. How will we ensure that participants understand the scope, risks, and benefits of the study, and provide genuine consent?
3. What safeguards will we put in place to protect participants' rights and autonomy throughout the study?

Stopping Rules

1. Under what circumstances would we terminate the study early due to safety concerns or participant well-being?
2. How will we communicate this decision to participants, researchers, and relevant stakeholders?
3. What contingency plans do we have in place for unexpected events or emergencies that may require stopping the study?

Data-Monitoring Needs

1. What data monitoring tools and procedures will we use to ensure participant safety and well-being throughout the study?
2. How will we handle data anomalies, deviations from expected outcomes, or concerns about potential harm to participants?
3. Who will be responsible for reviewing and addressing data-related issues, and what are their roles and responsibilities?

These questions serve as a foundation for constructing an ethics and safety framework for this hypothetical Phase-1 protocol.
