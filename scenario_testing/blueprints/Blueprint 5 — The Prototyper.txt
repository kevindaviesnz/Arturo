I'll be happy to assist you in designing a folded cardboard insert for holding a 300ml glass jar centered in a 120mm cube box.

Design Objectives

1. Hold the 300ml glass jar securely within the 120mm cube box.
2. Absorb impact and reduce stress on the jar during physical handling.
3. Minimize movement of the jar within the insert.

Assumptions

–  The cardboard board will be folded to create a strong and rigid structure.
–  The flute (the corrugated texture) will provide additional stiffness and strength to the design.
–  The wall thicknesses will be optimized for balance between rigidity and weight reduction.

Design

The insert will consist of four foldable flaps, two at each side of the box, with a central support strip connecting them. This configuration allows for maximum stability while minimizing material usage.

1. Fold Pattern: The flaps will be folded in a zigzag pattern (also known as a "chevron" or "V-fold"), which provides excellent stiffness and helps to distribute stress evenly.
2. Wall Thicknesses:
–  Bottom and top walls: 3mm thick
–  Side walls: 5mm thick
–  Support strip: 4mm thick
3. Support System: The central support strip will be designed with a slight taper to provide additional stability for the jar.
4. Jar Support: The 300ml glass jar will be supported by two small protrusions, one at each side of the insert, which will prevent it from shifting or toppling.

Board Grade and Corrugation

For this design, I assume a medium-grade corrugated cardboard board with a flute pitch of approximately 38.1mm (a typical value for general-purpose packaging). This will provide sufficient stiffness and strength while maintaining reasonable weight and cost considerations.

Please note that these assumptions may need to be adjusted based on further testing or simulation results from physical drop tests. I recommend revising the design based on observed performance and stability during these tests.

Next Steps

To refine this design, we can conduct physical drop tests using the 120mm cube box with the proposed insert. This will help identify any weaknesses or areas for improvement in the design. Based on the results of these tests, we can iterate on the design to optimize its performance and stability.

Let me know if you have any further questions or if there's anything else I can assist you with.
