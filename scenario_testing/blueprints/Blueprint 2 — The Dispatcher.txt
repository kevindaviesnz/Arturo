Instruction   An order came in for two ceramic mugs, one boxed teapot and a card. Produce a pick list in bin order, state the box size and how the fragile items must be separated, and list what the packer must check before sealing.

Pick List for Order

I will create a pick list based on the provided information. Please note that I do not have any prior knowledge about the warehouse operations or the specific stock locations.

Bin Order
The items are picked in the following order
1. Ceramic mugs
2. Boxed teapot
3. Card

Box Size and Separation Instructions

–  The boxed teapot requires a 12" x 8" x 6" box to accommodate its fragile contents.
–  The ceramic mugs should be placed on top of the teapot, separated by at least 2 inches to prevent breakage.
–  The card can be placed in a separate bin or bag for easy access.

Packing Instructions

1. Ensure the boxed teapot is securely placed in the designated box size.
2. Carefully arrange the ceramic mugs on top of the teapot, ensuring they are separated by at least 2 inches.
3. Add the card to a separate bin or bag.
4. Seal the boxes and bags securely.

Pre-Sealing Checks

1. Verify that all items are properly placed in their designated bins or bags.
2. Ensure the boxed teapot is securely closed and the ceramic mugs are tightly arranged on top.
3. Check that the card is separate from the other items.
4. Confirm that the boxes and bags are sealed securely.

Please note that these instructions assume a standard packing process and may need to be adjusted based on specific warehouse operations procedures or stock location information not provided.
