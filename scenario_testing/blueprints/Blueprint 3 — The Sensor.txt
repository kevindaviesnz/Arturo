Based on the provided records from Workshop Luthier's inspection, here are the key findings that impact the instrument's value:

1. The instrument has a back length of 356mm, indicating it is a standard or near-standard size.
2. The two-piece back with medium flame suggests that the instrument is made from high-quality spruce, which is valued for its acoustic properties.
3. The old repaired crack in the upper treble bout, cleaned and stable, indicates that the instrument has been well-maintained and cared for over time.
4. The seam open 40mm on the bass side suggests that the backplate has been opened or re-glued at some point, potentially affecting the instrument's structural integrity.

Given these findings, the asking price should be influenced by:

–  The quality of the spruce used in the construction
–  The instrument's size and standardization
–  The condition of the backplate and seams

However, it is essential to note that the exact asking price cannot be determined without further information on market demand, comparable sales data, and other relevant factors.

To provide a buyer with accurate information, the following should be disclosed:

–  The presence of a repaired crack in the upper treble bout and the extent of the repair
–  The condition of the backplate and seams
–  The instrument's size and standardization
–  Any potential issues or concerns related to the instrument's construction

A clear and transparent description of these findings will help buyers make an informed decision when purchasing the instrument.
