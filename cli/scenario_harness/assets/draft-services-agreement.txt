DRAFT SERVICES AGREEMENT (synthetic sample for demonstration)

Parties: Tidewater Design Limited ("Supplier") and Kauri Street Bakery Limited
("Client").

1. Services. The Supplier will design and build a website for the Client as
   described in the proposal dated 1 March. The proposal is not attached.

2. Term. This agreement starts on signing and continues until the Services are
   complete.

3. Fees. The Client will pay NZ$18,000 plus GST. 50% is payable on signing and
   50% on completion. "Completion" is not defined.

4. Liability. The Supplier's total liability under this agreement is limited to
   NZ$500. The Client's liability is unlimited.

5. Intellectual property. All intellectual property in the website remains with
   the Supplier. The Client receives a licence to use the website while fees are
   paid up to date.

6. Changes. The Supplier may change the scope of the Services at any time by
   notice to the Client.

7. Delay. The Supplier is not liable for any delay, however caused.

8. Payment default. Overdue amounts bear interest at 5% per month.

9. Termination. The Supplier may terminate at any time on 7 days' notice. The
   Client may terminate only for the Supplier's unremedied material breach, on
   90 days' notice.

10. Disputes. Any dispute will be decided by the Supplier's managing director,
    whose decision is final.

11. Governing law. Not stated.
