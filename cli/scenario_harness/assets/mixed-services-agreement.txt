BOOKKEEPING SERVICES AGREEMENT (synthetic sample for demonstration)

Parties: Rimu Bookkeeping Limited ("Contractor") and Pohutukawa Cafe Limited
("Customer").

1. Services. The Contractor will provide monthly bookkeeping for the Customer as
   described in Schedule A, which is attached and forms part of this agreement.

2. Fees. The Customer will pay NZ$600 plus GST per month. The Contractor will
   invoice monthly and each invoice is payable within 20 working days.

3. Price changes. The Customer may reduce the monthly fee at any time by written
   notice. The Contractor may not increase the fee during the term.

4. Liability. The Contractor's liability under this agreement is unlimited. The
   Customer's total liability is limited to the fees paid in the previous month.

5. Confidentiality. Each party will keep the other's confidential information
   confidential, except where disclosure is required by law.

6. Records. The Contractor will keep the Customer's records for an appropriate
   period.

7. Termination. The Customer may terminate at any time, immediately and without
   giving a reason. The Contractor may terminate only on six months' written
   notice.

8. Staff. Neither party will employ the other's staff during the term or for six
   months after it ends without the other party's written consent.

9. Disputes. The parties will refer any dispute to a mediator they agree on
   before either starts court proceedings.

10. Insurance. The Contractor will hold insurance.

11. Governing law. This agreement is governed by New Zealand law.

This is a synthetic sample. The parties are invented. It is not legal advice
and must be reviewed by a qualified person before any real use.
