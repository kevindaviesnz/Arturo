BALANCED SERVICES AGREEMENT (synthetic sample for demonstration)

Parties: Tidewater Design Limited ("Supplier") and Kauri Street Bakery Limited
("Client").

1. Services. The Supplier will design and build a website for the Client as
   described in Schedule 1, which is attached and forms part of this agreement.

2. Term. This agreement starts on signing and continues until the Services are
   complete or it is terminated under clause 9.

3. Fees. The Client will pay NZ$18,000 plus GST. 50% is payable on signing and
   50% on completion. "Completion" means the Client's written acceptance of the
   website, which the Client must not unreasonably withhold.

4. Liability. Each party's total liability to the other under this agreement is
   limited to the fees paid or payable under it. Neither party limits its
   liability for fraud.

5. Intellectual property. On payment of the fees in full, all intellectual
   property in the website passes to the Client. Each party keeps what it owned
   before this agreement.

6. Changes. The scope of the Services may be changed only by written agreement
   signed by both parties, which must state any change to the fees or timing.

7. Delay. Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its
   reasonable control, provided it tells the other party promptly and takes
   reasonable steps to limit the delay.

8. Payment default. Overdue amounts bear interest at 1% per month, and the
   Supplier may suspend work on 10 working days' written notice while an amount
   is overdue.

9. Termination. Either party may terminate on 30 days' written notice. Either
   party may terminate immediately if the other commits a material breach and
   does not remedy it within 14 days of being asked to.

10. Disputes. The parties will first try to resolve any dispute by discussion
    between their directors, and then by mediation, before either starts court
    proceedings.

11. Governing law. This agreement is governed by New Zealand law and the New
    Zealand courts have non-exclusive jurisdiction.

This is a synthetic sample. The parties are invented. It is not legal advice
and must be reviewed by a qualified person before any real use.
